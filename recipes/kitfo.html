Description

Kitfo is a traditional Ethiopian dish made from finely minced beef, seasoned with mitmita (a spicy chili blend) and niter kibbeh (spiced clarified butter). It is known for its rich, buttery flavor and can be served raw, lightly cooked, or well-cooked, often with ayib (Ethiopian cottage cheese) and greens.

Ingredients

  • Finely minced beef
  • Niter kibbeh (Ethiopian spiced clarified butter)
  • Mitmita (spicy Ethiopian chili powder)
  • Salt
  • Ayib (Ethiopian cottage cheese), for serving
  • Gomen (seasoned collard greens), for serving

Steps

  1. Mince the beef finely.
  2. Melt the niter kibbeh in a pan over low heat.
  3. Add mitmita and salt to the butter and mix.
  4. Add the minced beef and stir well.
  5. Cook lightly until warmed through, or cook longer if you prefer it well-done.
  6. Serve with ayib and gomen on the side.
