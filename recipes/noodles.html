Noodles

Home

Description

Noodles are a simple and delicious dish made from soft noodles mixed with vegetables, soy sauce, and seasonings. They are quick to prepare, flavorful, and can be served with chicken, beef, or egg.

Ingredients

  • Noodles
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Cooking oil
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Bell pepper
  • Carrot
  • Soy sauce
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Boil water in a pot and add a little salt.
  2. Add the noodles and cook until soft.
  3. Drain the noodles and set them aside.
  4. Fry the onion, garlic, carrot, and bell pepper in a little oil.
  5. Add the noodles and soy sauce.
  6. Mix everything together and cook for 2-3 minutes.
  7. Add black pepper and serve hot.
