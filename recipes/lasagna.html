Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a rich, comforting Italian dish made with layers of tender pasta sheets, savory meat or tomato sauce, creamy cheese, and melted mozzarella. Each bite combines hearty, flavorful sauce with soft pasta and gooey, golden cheese, making it a warm and satisfying classic.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna pasta sheets
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce or crushed tomatoes
  • Tomato paste
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Ricotta cheese (or cottage cheese)
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Cook the beef.
  2. Add tomato sauce and seasonings.
  3. Boil the lasagna sheets.
  4. Layer pasta, meat sauce, and cheese in a dish.
  5. Repeat the layers.
  6. Add cheese on top.
  7. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 30-40 minutes.
  8. Let it cool for a few minutes, then serve.
